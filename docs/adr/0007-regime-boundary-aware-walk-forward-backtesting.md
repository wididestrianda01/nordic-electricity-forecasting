# Walk-forward backtesting with explicit regime-boundary folds and a purge window

A naive walk-forward split would let folds straddle the Nov 2024 (flow-based coupling) or Oct 2025 (15-min MTU) boundaries, silently blending pre/post-break dynamics into a single fold's error. We chose expanding-window walk-forward where every fold is explicitly bounded against these two known break dates, with a purge window at least as long as the forecast horizon between train and test to prevent leakage. Evaluation metrics are CRPS, pinball loss, and coverage — not MAPE/RMSE, which are meaningless or misleading near zero/negative prices. This is more setup than a standard walk-forward split, but a naive split would understate error exactly at the transitions the project's headline result (ADR-0004) is built to explain.
